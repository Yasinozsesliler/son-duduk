You've never played football from this angle: the whistle is yours.

Refmania is a career simulator that brings football refereeing to life in full detail. You take charge of your first match in a regional amateur league; with the right decisions you climb the tiers, reach your country's top flight, earn your international badge and get appointed to the biggest matches in the world.

RUN THE MATCH
• Live 3D matches: players run, pass and shoot; you take up position.
• Watch contacts from close-up, referee's-eye and tactical cameras, and replay them in slow motion.
• Your angle of view affects your decision: standing in the right place is part of the job.

INTERPRET EVERY INCIDENT
• 16 incident types: penalty-area contact, handball, offside, elbows, denying a goal-scoring opportunity, fouls on the goalkeeper and more.
• Check the rulebook, ask your assistant, go to the video review or stand by your call.
• Card animations, protests, match management and stoppage-time decisions.

BUILD YOUR CAREER
• 8 countries with 5 tiers each; offers from abroad.
• An assessor's mark, a decision breakdown and press reaction after every match.
• The Referees Committee is watching: succeed and get the big matches; make mistakes and face a hearing.
• Weekly training: fitness, video analysis, laws seminars, mental preparation.

THE ENVELOPE IS ON THE TABLE
• Match-fixing offers arrive: turn them down, report them to the federation or take the envelope.
• Every envelope you take grows suspicion; investigations, suspensions and a lifetime ban may await.

SHOP AND HOLIDAYS
• Whistle, referee watch, boots, communication kit: equipment that boosts your performance on the pitch.
• A home, a car, watches and more; flashy spending brings followers but raises questions too.
• Go on holiday during the winter break and at the end of the season, and complete your postcard collection.

THE WORLD IS TALKING ABOUT YOU
• Fans, former referees and journalists debate your decisions on social media.
• News feed, league tables and calendar.
• Test yourself with tough incidents in the Laws Academy.

3 career slots, referee types and country choice. English, German, Spanish and Turkish. No internet connection required.

The game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Note: all teams, players and referees in the game are fictional.
