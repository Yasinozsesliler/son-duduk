You've never played football from this angle: the whistle is yours.

Refmania is a referee career simulator that lets you live every detail of officiating. Start in a regional amateur league, earn promotions with the right calls, reach your country's top flight, win your international badge and get appointed to the biggest matches in the world.

RUN THE GAME
• Live 3D matches broadcast like on TV: players run, pass and shoot while you take your position.
• Watch contacts from close-up, referee's-eye and tactical cameras, and replay them in slow motion.
• Your angle of view affects your call: being in the right place is part of the job.

EVERY INCIDENT IS YOUR CALL
• 16 incident types: penalty-area contact, handball, offside, elbows, denying a goal-scoring opportunity, goalkeeper challenges and more.
• Check the rulebook, ask your assistant, go to the VAR room or stand by your decision.
• Card animations, player protests, extra time and penalty shootouts in knockout matches.

DAILY POSITION
• Every day, everyone gets the same controversial incident. You have 40 seconds to decide. Share your call.

BUILD YOUR CAREER
• 8 countries with 5 levels each, plus offers from abroad.
• An observer rating, a decision breakdown and press reaction after every match.
• The Referees' Committee is watching: succeed and get the big games, make mistakes and get called in.
• A weekly training plan: fitness, video analysis, rules seminars and mental preparation.
• Bribe offers: refuse, report them to the federation or accept and risk an investigation and a ban.

THE WORLD IS TALKING ABOUT YOU
• Fans, former referees and journalists debate your calls on social media.
• News feed, league tables and fixtures.
• Test yourself with tough scenarios in the Rules Academy.

New to refereeing? Comfort mode gives you more time to decide and unlimited rulebook access.
