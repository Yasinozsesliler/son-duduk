An update shaped by player feedback
• More time to decide: 30 s on Normal and 38 s on Easy, or turn on "Untimed decisions" in Settings.
• Live match rating: your rating stays on screen and moves up or down with every call.
• Clearer added time: lost time is counted during the match, and the board shows the total.
• Clearer pitch: the broadcast camera is closer and players have team-coloured rings.
• Your assistant has your back: in matches without VAR, they flag a key mistake over the radio and you can check the replay again.
• Crowd pressure: whistles rise in tense moments and after wrong calls. Turn the crowd down or off in Settings.
• Road to Europe: a progress bar on the home screen shows how close you are to the European stage and what comes next.
