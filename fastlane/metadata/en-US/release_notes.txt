New modes
• 3 Seconds: The same 3 incidents for everyone, every day. The replay freezes at the moment of contact and you have 3 seconds to decide. Share your 🟩🟥 result card and keep your streak going.
• VAR room: Step into the booth and watch from 5 cameras. Confirm the on-field call with "Check complete", or intervene on a clear error and recommend the right decision.
• Referee locker: 13 shirts, 8 pairs of boots and new whistle tones. Unlock them by moving up divisions, with in-game cash or with Premium, and wear your pick in matches and replays.
• Improved English, German and Spanish translations.
