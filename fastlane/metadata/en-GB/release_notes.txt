Phone and Premium update
• The phone now has back, home and recent-apps buttons; swipe from the edge to go back. Taps respond much faster.
• At half-time your phone buzzes: fans react to your first-half calls on Kadraj.
• New: Weekly Premium mission, a special match with a cash reward every week.
• New: Replay studio. Super slow motion, frame-by-frame review and share your call as a clip.
• New: Personal rules coach, 5 weekly cases from your weakest incident types.
• A free weekly try of one legendary match and the Referee Report.
• Founding Referee badge: for everyone who goes Premium before 28 February.
• End-of-season deal and deal reminders (can be turned off in Settings).

New modes
• 3 Seconds: The same 3 incidents for everyone, every day. The replay freezes at the moment of contact and you have 3 seconds to decide. Share your result card and keep your streak going.
• VAR room: Step into the booth and watch from 5 cameras. Confirm the on-field call with "Check complete", or intervene on a clear error and recommend the right decision.
• Referee locker: 13 shirts, 8 pairs of boots and new whistle tones. Unlock them by moving up divisions, with in-game cash or with Premium, and wear your pick in matches and replays.
